# BINA Cost Tracker — Video Narration Script

**Format:** Voiceover narration with screen direction cues
**Tone:** Professional, corporate
**Target Audience:** Construction firms & property developers (Malaysia)
**Estimated Duration:** 1.5 – 2 minutes

---

## SCENE 1 — Opening Hook
**Duration:** ~15 seconds
**On Screen:** Montage of spreadsheets, paper blueprints, someone manually counting items on a drawing. Slow, tedious feel.

**NARRATION:**

> "In construction, cost estimation has always been one of the most time-consuming tasks. Manually counting elements, cross-referencing rate books, building spreadsheets from scratch — it takes days. And even then, one design change means starting all over again."

---

## SCENE 2 — Introducing BINA Cost Tracker
**Duration:** ~10 seconds
**On Screen:** BINA logo appears. Then transition to a Revit model open on screen with the BINA tab visible in the ribbon.

**NARRATION:**

> "BINA Cost Tracker changes that. Built directly into Autodesk Revit, it turns your BIM model into a live cost estimate — in just a few clicks."

---

## SCENE 3 — Step 1: Open Your Model
**Duration:** ~10 seconds
**On Screen:** User opens a Revit project file. The model loads — a multi-storey building with walls, doors, windows, floors visible.

**NARRATION:**

> "Start by opening your Revit model — the same model your team is already working on. No separate software. No file conversion. Just your existing BIM project."

---

## SCENE 4 — Step 2: Click Cost Tracker
**Duration:** ~10 seconds
**On Screen:** Cursor moves to the BINA tab in the Revit ribbon. Clicks the "Cost Tracker" button. The cost dashboard panel slides open on the right side of the screen.

**NARRATION:**

> "Click the Cost Tracker button in the BINA tab — and the cost dashboard opens right inside Revit."

---

## SCENE 5 — Step 3: Elements Are Extracted
**Duration:** ~15 seconds
**On Screen:** The dashboard populates with data. Show the total items count increasing. Zoom into the breakdown — walls, doors, windows, floors, ceilings all listed with quantities (m², m, units). Show the level breakdown (Ground Floor, Level 1, Level 2).

**NARRATION:**

> "BINA automatically walks through your entire model — extracting every wall, door, window, floor, ceiling, and fixture. Each element is categorized by type and level, with quantities calculated directly from the model. Area in square metres, lengths in metres, and item counts — all accurate, all automatic."

---

## SCENE 6 — Step 4: Click Match Prices
**Duration:** ~15 seconds
**On Screen:** Cursor clicks the "Match Prices" button. A progress indicator shows matching in action. JKR codes appear next to items. The coverage percentage bar fills up towards 100%. Unit prices populate in the cost table.

**NARRATION:**

> "Now, click Match Prices. BINA's AI engine maps every element to JKR and CIDB standard rates — automatically. It uses a multi-layer matching system: exact code matching, learned mappings from your previous projects, and AI-powered semantic search. The result — up to one hundred percent pricing coverage, in seconds."

---

## SCENE 7 — Step 5: Done — Full Cost Breakdown
**Duration:** ~15 seconds
**On Screen:** Show the completed dashboard — grand total in RM displayed prominently. Toggle between "By Level" and "By Category" views. Expand a category to show individual items with unit prices and totals. Highlight the coverage percentage at 100%.

**NARRATION:**

> "And just like that — your full cost estimate is ready. See the total project cost broken down by level or by category. Drill into any section to review individual items, unit prices, and quantities. Everything is transparent, traceable, and tied directly to your BIM model."

---

## SCENE 8 — Bonus: Export & Share
**Duration:** ~10 seconds
**On Screen:** Click "Export" button. An Excel file opens showing a professionally formatted cost report — headers, colour-coded columns, auto-calculated totals.

**NARRATION:**

> "Need to share it with your team or client? Export to Excel in one click — a professional cost report, ready to present."

---

## SCENE 9 — Closing & Call to Action
**Duration:** ~15 seconds
**On Screen:** Quick recap montage — Open model, click Cost Tracker, elements extracted, Match Prices, done. End on BINA logo with tagline and contact details.

**NARRATION:**

> "Four clicks. From BIM model to full cost estimate. No more manual takeoffs. No more guesswork. Just accurate, JKR-compliant costing — powered by AI, built for Malaysian construction."
>
> "This is BINA Cost Tracker. Ready to transform how you estimate?"

---

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Music:** Subtle, professional background track. Builds slightly during the AI matching scene (Scene 6), resolves to calm at the close.
- **Pacing:** Allow 1–2 seconds of breathing room between scenes for visual transitions.
- **Screen Recording Tips:**
  - Use a clean, well-modelled Revit project (multi-storey, multiple categories)
  - Ensure JKR codes are visible in element names for the matching scene
  - Record at 1080p minimum, cursor movements should be smooth and deliberate
  - Highlight UI elements with subtle zoom or spotlight effects during key moments
- **Branding:** BINA logo and colour scheme throughout. Consistent lower-third for feature labels.
